### Past Highlight

I previously worked as a **Cyber Ranger** for **SANS Institute** on their **SROC Team** where I built custom cloud ranges to author specifications that were then used in their courses by students to complete labs, CTFs, and NetWars. 

To this day, my favorite project was leading the development of their **Stark Research Labs 3** for the Forensics folks. This was an enterprise-grade range with multiple subnets, firewalls, routers, a domain controller, an exchange server, many other servers, and dozens of workstations. Once built and deployed, folks from the Offensive Operations curriculum then went to town attacking the range. Many logs and artifacts were collected along the way and are included in forensics courses for analysis work. 

That time as an SROC Ranger with SANS is the inspiration for the Build-A-CyberBear Workshop.  Building ranges to author specs and putting all the parts together really felt like the OG Build-A-Bear Workshop experience.


### What do I love about DevOps, Security and the Cloud?

I specialize in **Leadership, DevSecOps, and Cloud Security**. And I will tell you, **I love the DevOps Culture**. It’s really all about bringing people and teams together to solve shared goals and problems. I work with software engineers, cloud engineers, architects, product managers, and leadership to bridge the gaps across teams in building security and reliability into their processes, workflows, pipelines, and culture. I focus on operational excellence, reliability, resilience, and security-by-design. 

I like to say that I am a "student of the game".  I am always learning, relearning, practicing, and honing my craft.

To me, "The Cloud" means availability of resources for everyone.  Anyone can build, create and innovate without the need for purchasing or acquiring anything... No supercomputers required.  Anyone with an internet connection and a credit card can get access to all of the different resources and services that the cloud has to offer and build whatever their hearts desire. 

Folks anywhere can use these services.  It is pay as you go and low cost with minimal limits. The only limitations really are your own imagination for what you can create.
