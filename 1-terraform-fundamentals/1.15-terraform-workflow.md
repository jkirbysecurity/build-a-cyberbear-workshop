# 1.15 - Common Terraform Workflow

- `terraform init` – initiate configuration

- `terraform validate` – confirm configuration is valid (often skipped)

- `terraform plan` – run and review your plan

- `terraform apply` – deploy your range

- `terraform destroy` – destroy destroy destroy → do not forget to destroy when you are just playing around and/or testing – seriously, this is often what costs you $$$
