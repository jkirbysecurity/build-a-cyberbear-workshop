# 3.7 - Clean-Up

### Objectives

The objective of this lesson is to **destroy your range** and **clean-up/delete any manually created resources** in the console.


## Challenges

### Challenge 1: Destroy Range

1. From your terminal, run `terraform destroy`

2. When prompted for a response, type `yes` -- this is the only acceptable option


### Challenge 2: Delete Manual VPC and Other Resources

1. Delete your **EC2 Instance** `manual-web-server` now if you did not previously delete it

    - See [Lesson 2.5 Build Manually First -- EC2 Instances](/2-building-ranges-aws/2.5-manual-build-instances.md) --> **Challenge 3: Delete EC2 Instance and Security Group** for directions

2. Delete **VPC**

    - Select `manual-range-vpc`
    - Click **Actions** --> **Delete**

        ![VPC - Delete](/3-other-aws-resources/images/30-vpc-delete.png)

        ---

    - **Review** everything being deleted, type **delete** in the field, and then click **Delete**

        ![VPC - Confirm Delete](/3-other-aws-resources/images/31-vpc-confirm-delete.png)

        ---

    - Your `manual-range-vpc` should be gone from the **VPC list** now, and the only VPC showing should be the default VPC


3. Click through all the services in the left column to confirm everything deleted.  The only resources you should see are the defaults.  If any resources are left hanging, just delete them.

    - **Subnets**
    - **Route tables**
    - **Internet gateways**
    - **Security groups**


### Challenge 3: Delete S3 Bucket

1. Go back to the Console Home Page by selecting the **AWS** logo at the top left corner of the screen, and then select **S3** in the **Console Home** section.

    ![AWS Home Page](/3-other-aws-resources/images/40-go-to-s3.png)

    ---

2. AWS does not allow you to delete a bucket that is **NOT** empty.  Go into the bucket and delete objects.

    ![S3 - Select Bucket](/3-other-aws-resources/images/41-s3-select-bucket.png)

    ---

    - Select **objects** and click **Delete**

        ![S3 - Delete Objects](/3-other-aws-resources/images/42-s3-delete-objects.png)

        ---

    - Type `permanently delete` to confirm and click **Delete objects**

        ![S3 - Delete Objects - Confirm](/3-other-aws-resources/images/43-s3-delete-object-confirm.png)

        ---

4. Once the bucket is empty, select the bucket and Click **Delete**

    ![S3 - Delete](/3-other-aws-resources/images/44-s3-delete.png)

    ---

- Copy/paste the bucket name to confirm, and select **Delete bucket**


    ![S3 - Confirm Delete](/3-other-aws-resources/images/45-s3-delete-confirm.png)

    ---
